Your paintings

    Your face is the brush.

    To paint, the game needs to watch how your head and face move. Your browser will ask to use the camera next.

    • Each video frame is analyzed by a face-tracking model inside this browser tab, then discarded.
    • Only numbers, like how open your mouth is, are kept with your painting.
    • Before the round you can choose whether to record your face for a replay. That video stays in this browser session and is never uploaded.
    • The camera turns off when the round ends, or whenever you choose “Turn off and exit”.

        Record a face replay?

        Your camera video can be recorded for the replay, but it stays in this browser session and is never uploaded.

        • At the end you’ll watch your face and your painting side by side, in sync.
        • Recording starts with the round timer and stops when the round ends. A red “Recording” label shows the whole time.
        • The video is held in memory only. It’s gone when you delete it, start a new game, leave, or reload the page.
        • Your painting is saved as lightweight JSON either way.

        This browser can’t record video here, so the replay will show your painting only.
